LIBRARY RULES
Institute of Technology - Central Library

Clause 9.1 Borrowing
An undergraduate student may borrow up to four books at one time. A
postgraduate student may borrow up to six.

Clause 9.4 Overdue
The late return fine is 5 rupees per day per book. Borrowing privileges are
suspended once the accumulated fine exceeds 500 rupees.

Clause 9.7 Reference section
Reference volumes and bound journals may not be removed from the library
under any circumstance.
