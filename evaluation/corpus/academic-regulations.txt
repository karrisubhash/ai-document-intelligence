ACADEMIC REGULATIONS R20
Institute of Technology - Office of the Registrar

Clause 4.2 Attendance
A student must maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance in each course to
be eligible for the end-semester examination. Attendance is computed up to the
last instructional day of the semester.

Clause 4.3 Condonation
A student with attendance between 65 and 75 percent may apply for condonation
on medical grounds, supported by a certificate from a registered practitioner.
Condonation below 65 percent is not permitted under any circumstance.

Clause 5.1 Grading
A student who secures less than 40 percent in the end-semester examination is
declared to have failed that course, irrespective of internal marks.

Clause 6.4 Revaluation
An application for revaluation must be submitted within 15 days of the
publication of results, accompanied by the prescribed fee.
