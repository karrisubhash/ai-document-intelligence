PLACEMENT AND INTERNSHIP POLICY
Institute of Technology - Training and Placement Cell

Clause 1.3 Registration
A student must register with the cell before 15 August of the final year.
Registration is not automatic, and a student who has not registered is not
forwarded to any recruiter.

Clause 2.1 Eligibility
A student with an aggregate below 60 percent, or with a pending backlog in
any course, is not eligible for campus placement. Backlogs cleared before
the recruiter's own cut-off date are disregarded.

Clause 2.5 One-offer rule
A student who accepts an offer is withdrawn from further placement activity.
This applies from the moment the offer is accepted in writing, not from the
date of joining.

Clause 3.2 Internship credit
A summer internship of at least 8 weeks may be credited towards the degree,
subject to a report countersigned by the host organisation.

Clause 4.4 Deferral
A student who defers an accepted offer without the cell's written consent is
barred from campus placement for the remainder of the academic year.

Clause 5.1 Records
Placement statistics are published in aggregate only. Individual offer
details, including compensation, are not disclosed to other students.
