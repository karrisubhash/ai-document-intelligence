HOSTEL REGULATIONS
Institute of Technology - Office of the Warden

Clause 3.1 Curfew
The hostel curfew is 10 pm on weekdays and 11 pm on Saturdays. Residents
returning later must have written permission from the warden.

Clause 3.6 Visitors
Visitors are permitted in the common room only, between 4 pm and 7 pm.

Clause 8.2 Vacating
A resident vacating mid-semester forfeits the hostel fee for the remainder
of that semester.
