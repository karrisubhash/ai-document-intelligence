STUDENT GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL PROCEDURE
Institute of Technology - Office of the Dean of Students

Clause 1.2 Scope
This procedure covers grievances relating to academic assessment, hostel
accommodation, harassment and the conduct of staff. Grievances about the
result of a revaluation are outside its scope and are dealt with under the
academic regulations.

Clause 2.1 First stage
A grievance must be submitted in writing to the Dean of Students within 30
days of the incident complained of. A grievance submitted after that period
is admitted only where the Dean records reasons in writing.

Clause 2.3 Acknowledgement
The office acknowledges receipt within 3 working days and appoints a
committee within 10 working days.

Clause 3.1 Hearing
The committee hears the complainant in person. A complainant may be
accompanied by one member of the institute community, but not by an external
legal representative.

Clause 3.4 Decision
The committee issues its decision within 45 days of appointment. Where it
cannot, it records the reason and informs the complainant of a revised date.

Clause 4.2 Appeal
An appeal against a committee decision lies to the Director, and must be
lodged within 15 days of the decision being communicated.
