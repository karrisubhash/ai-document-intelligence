CIRCULAR TR/2024/117
Institute of Technology - Transport Section
Subject: Campus bus services for the academic year 2024-25

1. Routes and timings
Buses run on six city routes. On weekdays the first bus leaves the city
terminus at 7 am and the last bus leaves the campus at 7.30 pm. On Saturdays
the last bus leaves the campus at 2 pm. There is no service on Sundays.

2. Bus pass
A bus pass costs 4500 rupees per semester and is issued on payment at the
Transport Section. A student must show the bus pass together with the
institute identity card when boarding.

3. Late return of buses
Where a bus returns late to the campus because of an institute event, the
Transport Section informs the hostel office so that residents on that bus are
not marked as returning late.

4. Lost passes
A lost pass is replaced on payment of 200 rupees. A pass is not
transferable, and a pass found in use by another person is cancelled without
refund.

This circular supersedes circular TR/2023/094.
