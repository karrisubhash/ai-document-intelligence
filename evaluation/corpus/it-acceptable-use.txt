INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Institute of Technology - Computer Centre

Clause 1.1 Accounts
Every student is issued one institute account. Sharing account credentials
with another person is prohibited, and the account holder remains responsible
for all activity under the account.

Clause 1.4 Passwords
A password must be at least 12 characters and is changed every 180 days. The
Computer Centre never asks for a password by electronic mail, and any message
that does so should be reported.

Clause 2.2 Network quota
Each student account carries a monthly data allowance of 50 gigabytes.
Beyond the allowance, access continues at a reduced speed rather than being
cut off.

Clause 2.6 Prohibited use
Operating a commercial service, running a network scanner, or attempting to
access another user's files is prohibited. Access privileges are suspended
immediately on detection, pending an inquiry.

Clause 3.3 Laboratory computers
Files stored on laboratory computers are deleted at the end of each semester
and are not backed up. Work of lasting value is the student's own
responsibility to preserve.

Clause 4.1 Printing
The printing allowance is 200 pages per semester. Additional pages are
charged at 2 rupees per page.
