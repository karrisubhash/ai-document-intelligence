EXAMINATION POLICY
Institute of Technology - Controller of Examinations

Clause 12.1 Malpractice
A candidate found using unauthorised material in an examination hall is
debarred from all examinations of that semester.

Clause 12.5 Supplementary examination
A supplementary examination is conducted once per academic year, in July.

Clause 14.2 Identity
A candidate must carry the institute identity card into the examination hall.
Entry is refused without it.
