SCHOLARSHIP AND FINANCIAL AID RULES
Institute of Technology - Office of Student Welfare

Clause 2.1 Merit scholarship eligibility
A student who secures an aggregate of 80 percent or above in the preceding
academic year is eligible for the merit scholarship. The award covers 50
percent of tuition for the following year.

Clause 2.4 Attendance condition
A scholarship holder must maintain a minimum of 85 percent attendance. This
threshold is higher than the examination eligibility requirement, and a
scholarship is withdrawn for the remainder of the year if it is not met.
Condonation granted for examination purposes does not restore a scholarship.

Clause 3.2 Means-tested aid
A student whose annual family income is below 300000 rupees may apply for
means-tested aid, which covers the examination fee in full and up to 40
percent of hostel charges.

Clause 3.5 Application window
Applications for all financial aid close on 30 September. Late applications
are not considered, and there is no provision for condonation of this date.

Clause 4.1 Simultaneous awards
A student may not hold a merit scholarship and means-tested aid at the same
time. Where a student qualifies for both, the more favourable award applies.
