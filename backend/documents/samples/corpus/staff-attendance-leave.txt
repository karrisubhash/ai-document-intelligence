STAFF ATTENDANCE AND LEAVE RULES
Institute of Technology - Office of the Registrar, Establishment Section

Clause S1.1 Recording attendance
Attendance of teaching and non-teaching staff is recorded on the biometric
terminal at the main gate. A staff member records attendance on arrival and
again on departure. Attendance recorded at any other terminal is not counted.

Clause S1.2 Working hours
Working hours are 9 am to 5 pm on weekdays and 9 am to 1 pm on the first and
third Saturday of each month. Attendance on a working Saturday is recorded in
the same way as on a weekday.

Clause S1.3 Late arrival
Arrival after 9.15 am is recorded as late attendance. Three late attendances
in one calendar month are treated as half a day of casual leave. Late
attendance is not condoned on any ground other than an institute duty
recorded in advance.

Clause S2.1 Minimum attendance for confirmation
A staff member on probation must record a minimum of 90 percent attendance
during the probation period to be considered for confirmation. Attendance
below that minimum extends the probation by six months.

Clause S2.4 Examination duty
Staff assigned to examination duty record attendance at the examination hall
rather than at the main gate. Absence from an assigned examination duty
without a substitute approved by the Controller of Examinations is treated as
unauthorised absence, and the duty allowance is not paid.

Clause S3.1 Casual leave
Each staff member is entitled to 12 days of casual leave in a calendar year.
Casual leave cannot be combined with earned leave and cannot be carried
forward.

Clause S3.3 Earned leave
Earned leave accrues at 2.5 days per completed month of service and may be
accumulated up to 300 days. An application for earned leave must reach the
head of department at least 7 days in advance.

Clause S3.6 Leave during examinations
No leave other than medical leave is sanctioned to staff during the
end-semester examination period, except with the written approval of the
Director.

Clause S4.2 Attendance registers for hostel and library staff
Wardens, hostel attendants and library staff on shift duty maintain a manual
attendance register in addition to the biometric record. The register is
countersigned weekly by the warden or the librarian, as the case may be.

Clause S4.5 Deductions
Unauthorised absence results in a deduction of one day's pay for each day of
absence. Deductions are shown separately on the monthly pay slip and are not
refunded if leave is later applied for.

Clause S5.1 Records
Attendance records of staff are retained for 5 years and are open to the
staff member concerned on request to the Establishment Section.
